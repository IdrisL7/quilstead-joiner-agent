Every new joiner receives a laptop, a charger and a headset. Engineers receive a 14 inch MacBook Pro or a ThinkPad X1 by preference; everyone else receives a 13 inch MacBook Air by default.

IT orders equipment within five working days of the contract being signed. Office and hybrid joiners collect their equipment from the office reception on their first morning. Remote joiners have it shipped to their home address at least three working days before the start date, with a tracking number recorded in the case.

Monitors, keyboards and desk equipment for home working are requested by the manager after the first week.
