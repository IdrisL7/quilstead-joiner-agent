Office and hybrid joiners arrive at reception at 9:30 on their first day, collect their equipment, and are met by their buddy. Remote joiners join a video call with their buddy at 9:30 in their local time zone.

The manager owns the first-day plan: a welcome conversation, team introductions, and one small, real piece of work to finish in the first week. The manager confirms the plan to the People team by the Wednesday before the start date. If the manager is on leave, their named deputy owns the plan.

Compliance training is assigned on day one and completed within the first two weeks.
