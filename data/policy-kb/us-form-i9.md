Every employee of Quilstead Solutions Inc completes Section 1 of Form I-9 no later than their first day of work. Section 2 is completed by the People team within three business days of the start date, after reviewing the original documents in person or through the approved remote verification process.

The joiner also completes federal and state tax withholding forms in the first week. The People Partner for the US owns both items and records completion in the HRIS.

If Section 2 cannot be completed within three business days, the People Partner for the US is alerted the same day and the case is flagged for review.
