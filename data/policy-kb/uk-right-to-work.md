Every employee of Quilstead Solutions UK Ltd must have a completed right to work check before their first working day. The check is completed by the People team and recorded in the HRIS with the document type and the date it was verified.

Acceptable evidence includes a British or Irish passport, a share code for the online check, or a biometric residence permit. Copies are stored as identity documents in the HRIS; they are never attached to emails or Slack messages.

If the check is not complete five working days before the start date, the People Partner for the UK is alerted and the start date is put on hold until the check is complete. No manager, buddy or system can override the hold.
