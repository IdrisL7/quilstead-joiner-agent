Every new joiner is paired with a buddy before their first day. A buddy is a colleague who has opted into the programme, has at least six months at Quilstead, is not on leave during the joiner's first two weeks, and is supporting fewer than two other new joiners at the time.

Buddies are chosen from the joiner's office where possible. For remote joiners, a buddy in the same country and time zone is acceptable. The buddy is not the joiner's manager and is usually not in the same reporting line.

The buddy introduces themselves before the start date, meets the joiner on the first morning, and checks in at the end of the first week. If no eligible buddy is available, the People Partner for the region arranges one by hand.
