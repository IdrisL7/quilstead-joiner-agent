Quilstead Solutions GmbH notifies the works council of every planned hire at least one week before the start date, with the role, the team and the planned start date. The People Partner for DACH sends the notice and records the date in the HRIS.

Before the first payroll run, the People team registers the joiner for social insurance and records the tax identification number and the social insurance number in the HRIS. The joiner supplies both in the pre-start form.

Joiners who relocate to Germany register their address with the local authority within fourteen days of moving. That registration is the joiner's own responsibility; Quilstead provides a checklist and a relocation contact but does not file it.
