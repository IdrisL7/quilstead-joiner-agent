title: Handling joiner personal data
country: ALL
owner: Security

---

Joiner personal data is held in the HRIS. Identity documents, bank details and compensation are visible only to the People and Finance roles that need them and are never copied into tickets, messages or automation logs.

Automation that acts on joiner data reads the minimum fields needed for the task, records what it read, and stores no document contents. Any message that leaves Quilstead, to a joiner or a third party, is approved by a named person before it is sent.

Joiner data for employees of Quilstead Solutions GmbH and Quilstead Solutions UK Ltd is processed in the EU and the UK. Requests to move it elsewhere go to Security.
