# UK joiner

- Right to work must be evidenced before the start date. At five working days before start with no evidence, escalate RTW_NOT_EVIDENCED to the UK People Partner. The agent cannot evidence it.
- HRIS profile completed within one day of the contract.
- Access requests filed at least two working days before start from the role matrix.
- Equipment ordered within five working days of the contract; remote joiners' kit ships three working days before start.
- Buddy allocated at least three working days before start, London office preferred.
- Manager confirms the day-one plan by the Wednesday before the start date.
- Welcome message drafted for approval one working day before start.
