# US joiner

- Form I-9 Section 1 by the first day; Section 2 within three business days of start. Both owned by the US People Partner; the agent tracks and escalates, it does not complete them.
- Federal and state tax forms in the first week.
- HRIS profile within one day of the contract.
- Access requests filed at least two working days before start from the role matrix.
- Equipment ordered within five working days of the contract; remote joiners' kit ships three working days before start.
- Buddy allocated at least three working days before start, Denver office preferred; same time zone for remote.
- Manager confirms the day-one plan by the Wednesday before the start date; the deputy owns it if the manager is on leave.
