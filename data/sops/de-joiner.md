# DE joiner

- Works council notice sent at least one week before the start date by the DACH People Partner. Compliance item; the agent tracks and escalates.
- Social insurance registration and tax identification number recorded before the first payroll; deadline set to the start date in this plan.
- Right to work: residence permit status must be evidenced before start for non-EU joiners; escalate at five working days.
- HRIS profile within one day of the contract.
- Access requests filed at least two working days before start from the role matrix.
- Equipment ordered within five working days of the contract.
- Buddy allocated at least three working days before start; Berlin or Munich office, same time zone for remote. If none eligible, escalate.
- Manager confirms the day-one plan by the Wednesday before the start date.
